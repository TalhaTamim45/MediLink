Frequently Asked Questions

You can track your order by navigating to the "Orders" tab in the bottom menu. Select your active order to see real-time updates on its status, from processing to delivery.

To upload a prescription, go to the Home screen and tap on "Upload Prescription". You can take a new photo using your camera or select an existing image/PDF from your gallery. Our pharmacists will review it within 30 minutes.

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), bKash, Nagad, and major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex).

Medicines can only be returned if they are damaged upon delivery or if the wrong item was sent. Please contact our support team within 24 hours of delivery with photographic evidence.
